E-Commerce SQL Analytics

A business-focused SQL analytics project** using the **Olist Brazilian E-Commerce Public Dataset** to analyze e-commerce sales, customer behavior, product performance, regional revenue, and delivery satisfaction.
The project addresses practical e-commerce business problems such as **understanding revenue trends, identifying high-performing products and regions, measuring customer retention, and evaluating how delivery performance relates to customer satisfaction**.


## Objectives
* Analyze overall revenue and monthly sales performance
* Calculate month-over-month revenue growth
* Identify top-performing product categories by revenue
* Analyze regional revenue across Brazilian states
* Measure customer retention through repeat purchase rate
* Evaluate the relationship between delivery performance and customer satisfaction

## SQL Concepts Used
* MySQL & Relational Database Design
* Primary & Foreign Keys
* Multi-table `JOIN` / `LEFT JOIN`
* Aggregate Functions (`COUNT`, `SUM`, `AVG`)
* `GROUP BY`, `ORDER BY`, `WHERE`
* `CASE` statements
* Subqueries & Conditional Aggregation
* Window Functions (`LAG`, `RANK`)
* Date & Percentage Calculations
* CSV Data Loading with `LOAD DATA INFILE`

## Dataset
Olist Brazilian E-Commerce Public Dataset — Kaggle
The analysis uses customer, seller, product, order, payment, and review data to answer practical business questions.
